Ricky's Herbs was founded by Erica Gilo, following her own frustrating skincare journey. After years of using processed products that only offered temporary fixes, Erica decided to look deeper. She discovered a massive gap in the market for genuine, natural herbal healthcare.

When she switched to raw, natural herbs, the results were transformative and undeniable. Within a remarkably short time, both her skin and gut health completely transformed.

Realizing that the body heals best from the inside out, Erica launched Ricky's Herbs to share these life-changing remedies with the world, and hopefully reach out to others who faced the same struggle with their health and wellness.
